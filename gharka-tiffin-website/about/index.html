Our Purpose & Heritage

Bringing Real Homestyle Comfort to Delhi's Fast-Paced Life

In a city running on commercial cloud kitchens, synthetic gravies, and reheated restaurant food, GharKa was born out of a single honest yearning: having real, comforting food that feels like it was cooked by your own mother.

How It All Began

Born in a Lajpat Nagar Kitchen in 2021

Our founders, Kabir and Neha, moved to Delhi from Punjab and Madhya Pradesh for corporate careers. Within months of 12-hour workdays, they faced the dilemma that millions of Delhiites confront daily: oily restaurant food led to acidity, canteen food lacked hygiene, and local cook-maids frequently skipped work without notice.

They partnered with Sunita Aunty, their beloved next-door neighbor in Lajpat Nagar whose slow-simmered Punjabi Rajma and soft ghee phulkas were legendary across the colony. What began as packing 10 lunchboxes for office colleagues soon grew through word of mouth into a dedicated network of home-cooks across Delhi NCR.

Today, GharKa delivers over 1,200 fresh tiffins every single day across South Delhi, Central Delhi, North Campus, Noida, and Gurgaon — without ever compromising on small-batch cooking, cold-pressed oils, or that irreplaceable feeling of home.

Uncompromised Standards

Our 6-Point Hygiene & Sourcing Promise

We cook the exact same food we feed our own children. Here are the sacred ground rules we follow every morning.

🥬

Daily Mandi Sourcing

We do not use frozen or cold-storage vegetables. Every morning at 5:00 AM, our team procures fresh seasonal produce directly from Okhla wholesale vegetable mandi.

🌾

Stone-Ground Chakki Atta

Zero maida, zero starch fillers. Our rotis are made from 100% whole grain wheat ground fresh in our stone mill, preserving vital bran fiber and micronutrients.

🫒

Cold-Pressed Oils & Cow Ghee

Strictly zero palm oil, zero hydrogenated fats. We cook exclusively with cold-pressed mustard oil, groundnut oil, and pure aromatic A2 desi cow ghee.

💧

100% RO Purified Cooking

Every drop of water used in boiling lentils, washing vegetables, and kneading dough passes through an industrial 5-stage RO + UV purification system.

♨️

85°C Steam-Sterilized Dabbas

All multi-tier stainless steel tiffin containers undergo automated hot steam sterilization to ensure 100% germ-free, odorless packing.

📜

FSSAI Certified & Audited

Fully licensed under FSSAI (Registration #13324005000123). Weekly random food safety and surface hygiene swab audits conducted by certified labs.
