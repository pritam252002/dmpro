Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026. Effective Immediately.

At GharKa (operated by GharKa Tiffin Services Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in New Delhi, India), we are deeply committed to respecting and safeguarding the personal privacy of our customers, website visitors, and subscribers. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent policies regarding the collection, processing, and handling of information when you interact with our website (gharka.in), mobile interfaces, or enquiry forms.

🌟 Our Golden Rule: We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your name, phone number, or delivery address to third-party telemarketers or ad brokers. Period.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to deliver wholesome meals to your doorstep, coordinate delivery timings, and process payments:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, mobile/WhatsApp telephone number, and email address submitted via our enquiry forms or WhatsApp chat.
  • Delivery Location: Your complete residential, hostel, PG, or corporate office delivery address, PIN code, and landmark instructions for our delivery fleet.
  • Meal Preferences & Dietary Notes: Your selected diet plan (e.g. Veg Thali, Jain Satvik, Light/Diet, Student), allergy warnings, and spice/salt instructions.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through Google Analytics 4 (GA4) cookies to improve site performance and usability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To dispatch hot lunch (12:00–1:30 PM) and dinner (7:30–9:00 PM) to your designated address in Delhi NCR.
  • To send you operational WhatsApp and SMS alerts regarding order confirmation, daily menu updates, pause/freeze acknowledgments, and renewal notices.
  • To respond promptly to customer service queries and dietary customization requests.
  • To analyze anonymous traffic patterns on our website using Google Analytics 4 to optimize mobile loading speed and user interface layout.

3. Cookies and Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

Our website uses standard cookies and Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with measurement ID G-XXXXXXXXXX. These cookies collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics (such as session duration, bounce rates, and lead conversion events like generate_lead).

You can configure your internet browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Refusing cookies will not affect your ability to browse our menu or contact us via WhatsApp or phone.

4. Data Sharing & Security Practices

We treat your personal details with the utmost care:

  • Third-Party Vendors: We only share necessary delivery information (name, address, phone number) with our verified in-house delivery personnel and logistics partners executing your meal drop-off.
  • No Spam Guarantee: We do not engage in automated spam robocalls or unsolicited third-party marketing SMS blasts.
  • Secure Storage: All data collected through web forms is stored on secure, encrypted cloud servers in accordance with Indian Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

5. Your Rights & Control Over Your Data

As a valued subscriber, you possess full control over your personal records:

  • You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you at any time.
  • You may request corrections or complete deletion of your customer profile, delivery address, and contact history upon concluding your subscription.
  • You can opt-out of promotional WhatsApp alerts by replying "STOP" or notifying our customer support team.
